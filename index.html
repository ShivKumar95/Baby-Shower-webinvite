Udaka Shanti

Key Significance and Benefits • Purifies space: Removes negative energy from homes and people. • Restores peace: Brings wellbeing and calm to households. • Invokes deities: Calls upon Lord Varuna and other devas for health and prosperity. • Clears doshas: Prepares spaces for major events.

12:00 PM

Valaikappu

• Bangle Arrangement: The expectant mother wears a traditional 9-yard saree. Family members fill her hands with an odd or even number of red and green glass bangles, starting with special bangles (such as neem leaves, gold, or silver kappu). The sound of the tinkling bangles is believed to stimulate the baby's developing sense of hearing. • Auspicious Offerings: Relatives serve an array of sweets, savories, and paruppu thengai (traditional lentil-coconut structures). • Blessings: Sumangalis (married women) apply sandalwood paste and kumkum on the mother's arms and face, blessing her with a smooth and healthy delivery.

1:00 PM

Seemantham

• The Homam: A family priest conducts a sacred fire ritual (Ayushya Homam or Sudarsana Homam) to invoke divine blessings for a safe delivery and a long, healthy life for the baby. • Hair Parting: The husband gently parts the expectant mother's hair using a mullupandri mul (porcupine quill) and a stalk of nellu (paddy grain). This ancient ritual is believed to stimulate the mother's nervous system and positively influence the fetus's brain development.

2:00 PM

Lunch

A warm family meal with traditional dishes, conversation, and time to celebrate together.

Deploy in Seconds to Netlify

This web app is completely self-contained in a single file and comes configured with Netlify Forms so you can gather RSVPs without any extra backend code.

Step 1: Save this file

Save this code as index.html in a new folder on your computer.

Step 2: Drag & Drop onto Netlify Drop

Open app.netlify.com/drop in your browser and drag the folder right into the browser.

Step 3: Collect RSVPs

Your site gets an instant live URL (e.g. baby-miller-shower.netlify.app). Every time a guest fills out the RSVP form, their response will show up inside your Netlify site dashboard under Forms > baby-shower-rsvp!
